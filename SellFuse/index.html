SellFuse

A seller and reseller workflow that turns item details into reviewed, destination-aware listing drafts and coordinated post-sale actions.

ProductSeller workflow SaaS candidate
StackTypeScript and Node.js
InterfacesWeb, API, and mobile entrypoint
AccessPrivate development

Problem

Sellers repeat the same item and listing work across destinations.

SellFuse centralizes item intake, user review, listing preparation, marketplace capability disclosure, and the mark-sold-once workflow. It keeps generated assistance separate from supplied market evidence and preserves a manual path when local inference is unavailable.

Architecture

Independent web, API, domain, integration, and self-hosted inference boundaries.

The implementation includes account authentication, product-scoped entitlements, validated item/listing models, an authenticated AI gateway, assisted marketplace handoffs, tests, containers, and CI. Persistence remains in memory and direct marketplace integrations are explicitly marked planned.

  1. Capture photos and seller-supplied facts.
  2. Prepare a canonical item and listing through validated domain services.
  3. Review destination-specific drafts and capability disclosures.
  4. Coordinate assisted publication and sold-item follow-up.

Membership

Potential structure

Free, Pro, and Autopilot entitlements are conceptually plausible, but final names, limits, and pricing require a product decision.

Billing status
