Buyer workspace · Private source · Product prototype

BuyFuse

A focused workspace for saving items, source links, notes, and purchase status without turning the buyer experience into a copy of the seller workflow.

CustomerIndividual buyers and researchers
StackTypeScript and Node.js
BoundaryIndependent runtime and repository
StatusPrototype

Problem

Purchase research is fragmented across tabs, notes, and screenshots.

BuyFuse gives a buyer one private place to record an item, its source, decision notes, and progress from saved to purchased. Product-scoped authorization keeps buyer mutations separate from seller permissions.

Architecture

A small service boundary designed to grow independently.

The current implementation includes a responsive server-rendered workspace, authentication, URL validation, rate-limited account flows, product entitlements, container configuration, tests, and a health endpoint. Identity and workspace persistence are still in memory.

  1. Authenticate against the BuyFuse issuer and audience.
  2. Authorize product-scoped buyer permissions.
  3. Save validated item, source-link, and note data.
  4. Track the purchase lifecycle inside the buyer workspace.

Business model

Do not force a standalone subscription yet.

Recommended direction

Start as a free or included companion experience. Re-evaluate transaction or membership economics only after durable accounts and a real network workflow exist.

Billing status

No billing or checkout infrastructure is implemented.

Launch requirement

Durable storage, production identity, privacy controls, monitoring, and an approved acquisition model remain required.
